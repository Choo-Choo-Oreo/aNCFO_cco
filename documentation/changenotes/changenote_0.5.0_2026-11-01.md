[h3]SAVE COMPATIBILITY[/h3]
Harmonainus character, idea, focus and balance of power IDs were renamed. Saves started on 0.4.2 or earlier may fail to load or lose Harmonainus content. Start a new game.

[h3]HARMONAINUS IMPERIOUS[/h3]
[list]
[*] The Harmonainus ruler is now Lucas Actius throughout, replacing Luke Deed. Reforms, decisions, peace conference rules, the game rule option and opinion modifiers follow the new name.
[*] Electors renamed: Avidius Cassius, Isabella Delphina and Archardus Vladarus. The Nobility leader is now Lucius Corbulo.
[*] Harmonainus ideology text, religion text and country names standardized.
[*] Spelling fixes: chaos ideas, Giants of the East bookmark, and several Harmonainus decisions and events.
[*] Divine Right Coring now checks its candidate states more reliably.
[/list]

[h3]BALANCE[/h3]
[list]
[*] Artillery brigade bonuses retuned: breakthrough reduced on standard artillery, support artillery now has a 25 percent soft attack bonus and positive breakthrough.
[*] Artillery equipment gains higher defense and breakthrough, with hard attack and armor piercing added on the heavier guns.
[*] Fire support battalions have more organisation and strength, and now require support and infantry equipment.
[*] Tank module breakthrough penalties reduced from -4 to -2.
[*] Faction war declaration and call to war penalties halved to match Hearts of Iron IV 1.19.4.
[*] Research facilities (air, land, naval) cost less to build up front but more per facility already controlled.
[/list]
